# Night Crossing

An arcade lane-crossing game. A toad crosses the edge of town at night, over
a busy road, a railway and a river, to five nooks on the far bank.

![The title screen, with the game playing itself behind it](docs/screenshots/title.png)

![Night on the road and the river](docs/screenshots/night.png)

## How to play

- Hop one cell at a time. Cars, vans, bikes and trucks squash you on the road.
- On the river, ride the logs, barges and the ferry. The water is deadly, and
  so is being carried off the edge.
- Lily pads dive when they flash. Ships sink, bow first: run to the stern or
  hop off. Never land on a heron.
- Fill all five nooks before the clock runs out to clear the level. After
  level 8 the last levels loop, faster each time round.
- **Fruit** shows in an empty nook now and then, from level 2: fill that
  nook while it's there for a bonus, a different fruit and more points each
  level.
- **Fireflies** fly in over the river from level 4, land on the logs (later
  the lily pads and boats too), flit about and fly off again. Land in a
  firefly's cell while it sits there to catch it for an extra life; you
  can't catch one in the air. By day, in the later modes, a dragonfly comes
  instead.

Points: 10 for each new row reached, 100 for a nook plus 10 for each second
left, 1000 for the fifth nook, and the fruit's points. An extra life every
20,000. You can hold up to 8 lives; at 8, a firefly is worth 500 points
instead.

## Modes

- **Stroll:** the game above.

Each later mode has everything before it from level 1, fruit and fireflies
included, and adds its own. In every later mode the road gets **mixed
traffic** from level 4: cars, bikes and vans join the trucks in the top
lane, the bottom lane mixes too from level 6, and all four lanes are mixed
from level 8. Big trucks stay in the top lane. Each lane keeps its speed and
its pattern, so it can still be learned.

From level 3 of every later mode, **predators** come out, each with a
warning:

- **The heron** walks along its log a cell at a time, turning at the ends.
  From level 6 it stops, cocks its head and flashes, then jabs the cell it
  faces.
- **The fox** works the nooks at dusk and night. First its eyes glint in a
  nook: hop in then and it slinks off. Once the fox is in, that nook is
  deadly until it goes.
- **The water rat,** from level 4. A ripple on the water, then it swims for
  the end of a log or a boat and grabs it. Don't wait on that end.
- **The snake,** from level 5. The verge grass rustles, then a snake slides
  back and forth along a stretch of it for a while. Don't land on it. It
  never comes out while the verge floods.

From level 6 two can be out at once: two water rats, or a water rat and the
snake.

- **Last Train:** Stroll, plus:
  - **Trains.** When the crossing lights flash and the bell rings, a train
    is coming: stay off the track until it has passed. Each level the
    trains get faster and come more often.
  - **A hop combo,** from level 2. Keep hopping forward for x2, x3 and x4
    points, on the hops, the nook and its fruit. Waiting too long, hopping
    back or dying ends it; riding a log or a boat keeps it.
  - **Day and night,** from level 3. Each level runs from dawn to night as
    you fill the nooks.
- **Rush Hour:** Last Train, plus the road in bad weather:
  - **Rush hour waves.** When a lane's traffic light turns amber, then red,
    a wave of traffic is coming down it. It always leaves a way across.
  - **Fog,** from level 2. It hides the cars inside it, but not their
    headlights.
  - **Rain,** from level 3. A hop that lands on the road slides one more
    cell the same way: plan for it.
  - **Emergency vehicles,** from level 3. Red and blue lights flash at the
    edge of one of the two middle lanes: "EMERGENCY VEHICLE: CLEAR THE
    LANE". Its cars hurry off, and the lanes either side of it bunch up and
    stop to let it through. Get out of that lane before the vehicle comes,
    or you're run down. The police car comes first, the ambulance from
    level 4 and the fire truck from level 5.
- **Storm:** Rush Hour, plus storms that roll in, darken the sky with
  lightning and thunder, and roll away again, with clear spells between:
  - **Currents.** Arrows on the water show a current. Standing on a log or
    a boat in that row, you slide along it: hop back before you slide off.
  - **Wet storms.** "STORM ROLLING IN: HEAVY RAIN": it rains all through,
    so a hop onto the road slides one more cell. From level 3, stretches of
    the verge show puddles, then go under, and the river runs faster until
    the storm rolls away.
  - **Dry storms,** from level 2. "STORM ROLLING IN: HIGH WINDS FROM THE
    WEST" (or east): the wind pushes you one cell that way every few
    seconds, with streaks building just before each push. Stand where a
    push is safe.
  - Neither kind is harder; which comes next is the luck of the weather.
    Rain and fog can come in the clear spells too.
- **School Run:** Storm from level 1, plus toadlets to walk across:
  - **The escort.** A toadlet follows your trail, one move behind: your
    hops, your slides in the rain and the wind's pushes. If anything gets
    it, you lose a life.
  - **Stop to gather them.** Stand still on the grass for a moment and the
    line catches up into your cell, and is pushed with you.
  - **The river.** Gather the line on the verge and the toadlets ride on
    your back over the water. Hop onto the river with one left behind and
    it peeps: you can't go yet.
  - **Home together.** Reach a nook and they all get home with you: +50
    for each toadlet, with your combo.
  - **Emergency vehicles.** The queues give way to your toadlet as they
    do to you, but the vehicle runs it down too. It follows one move
    behind, so hop on until it is out of the lane as well.

## Mastering a mode

Only Stroll is open at first. Clear a mode's level 8 and you play its
**bonus level**: no lives to lose (a slip just costs time and sends you back
to the start), one clock, and as much to collect as you can before it runs
out. Collect the whole set early for a bonus and 50 points a second left.

- **Firefly night** (Stroll): catch 10 fireflies among parked cars and on
  the logs.
- **The last train home** (Last Train): ride the carriages of a goods yard
  at dawn for 12 fruit, before the last train pulls out.
- **Empty roads at dawn** (Rush Hour): beat two street sweepers to 12 fruit
  on the road.
- **After the storm** (Storm): stay out on the logs under a rainbow; each
  dragonfly caught without touching land is worth more than the last.
- **The school gates** (School Run): gather the toadlets waiting at the
  kerbs, up to four in your line, and walk them home; three or more in one
  trip pays double. Then the game's ending.

Then the **MASTERED** screen: the mode is mastered and the next one opens.
**Finish** for 1,000 points for each life left, or **carry on** into the
looping levels for score. The high score tables mark mastered runs, and how
far a run carried on.

## Options

Fullscreen, sound, music and effects with their volumes, a sound test, reduced
motion, smooth or pixel light, glow, a CRT look and high contrast (outlined
hazards and a lighter night).

![The options menu](docs/screenshots/options.png)

Settings and high scores are saved as `settings.json` and `scores.json` next
to the game.

## Download

Builds for Windows and Linux (64-bit) are on the
[Releases](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/releases) page, with
`SHA256SUMS.txt` to check them against.

- **Windows:** unzip `nightcrossing-vX.Y.Z-windows-amd64.zip` and run
  `nightcrossing.exe`.
- **Linux:** extract `nightcrossing-vX.Y.Z-linux-amd64.tar.gz` and run
  `./nightcrossing`. It needs OpenGL, X11 and ALSA, which desktop systems
  already have.

Settings and high scores are saved in the folder the game is in, so keep it
somewhere you can write to.

## Building

Needs Go 1.27 or later.

```sh
go run ./cmd/nightcrossing                 # run the game
go run ./cmd/nightcrossing -seed 42        # with a fixed random seed
go vet ./...
go test ./...
```

**Windows** (no cgo):

```sh
CGO_ENABLED=0 GOOS=windows GOARCH=amd64 go build -ldflags "-H=windowsgui" -o bin/nightcrossing.exe ./cmd/nightcrossing
```

The window always shows the program icon. For Explorer to show it on the exe
too, link it in before building:

```sh
go run ./cmd/winicon nightcrossing.ico
go run github.com/akavel/rsrc@v0.10.2 -arch amd64 -ico nightcrossing.ico -o cmd/nightcrossing/rsrc_windows_amd64.syso
```

The `.syso` is only used by Windows amd64 builds.

**Linux** (amd64) needs cgo and the X11/GL/ALSA development packages. On
Debian or Ubuntu:

```sh
sudo apt-get install libgl1-mesa-dev libxcursor-dev libxi-dev libxinerama-dev libxrandr-dev libxxf86vm-dev libasound2-dev pkg-config
go build -o bin/nightcrossing ./cmd/nightcrossing
```

**For development:**

```sh
go run -tags debug ./cmd/nightcrossing             # debug overlay
go run ./cmd/nightcrossing -level 38               # start games on level 38 (no scores kept)
go run -tags debug ./cmd/nightcrossing -noshaders  # draw as if shaders were unavailable
go run -tags debug ./cmd/nightcrossing -storm dry  # every storm wet or dry (no scores kept)
go run -tags debug ./cmd/nightcrossing -bonus      # start on the chosen mode's bonus level (no scores kept)
go test ./internal/world -run TestStormBalance -balance -v  # deaths per storm, wet and dry
go generate ./assets                               # rebuild the sprite sheets
```
