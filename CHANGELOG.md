# Changelog

All notable changes to Night Crossing are listed here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and the project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.10.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.9.0...v1.10.0) - 2026-10-18

### Added

- Emergency vehicles in Rush Hour, Storm and School Run: the police car from
  level 3, the ambulance from level 4 and the fire truck from level 5. The
  lanes either side bunch up and stop, a trap for a toad left in the lane.
- School Run's toadlets are given way to by the queues, and can be run down
  by the vehicle.

### Changed

- School Run escorts one toadlet on every level.

## [1.9.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.8.0...v1.9.0) - 2026-10-18

### Added

- Predators from level 3 of every mode but Stroll, each with a warning: the
  heron stalks its log and strikes, the fox works the nooks, the water rat
  grabs the ends of platforms and the snake patrols the verge.

## [1.8.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.7.0...v1.8.0) - 2026-10-18

### Added

- Mixed traffic on the road from level 4 of every mode but Stroll.

## [1.7.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.6.0...v1.7.0) - 2026-10-18

### Added

- A bonus level for each mode: firefly night, the last train home, empty
  roads at dawn, after the storm and the school gates.
- Mastering a mode opens the next one. School Run's bonus level leads to the
  ending and the credits.

## [1.6.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.5.0...v1.6.0) - 2026-10-18

### Added

- The flying firefly, dragonflies by day and fruit to pick up, with their
  own sounds.

## [1.5.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.4.0...v1.5.0) - 2026-10-17

### Added

- School Run mode: walk a line of toadlets across, with their own sprites,
  peeps and cries.

## [1.4.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.3.1...v1.4.0) - 2026-10-17

### Changed

- Storm reworked: storms roll in and away under a new storm sky.

## [1.3.1](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.3.0...v1.3.1) - 2026-10-17

### Changed

- Storm's storm front, with wet and dry storms.

## [1.3.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.2.0...v1.3.0) - 2026-10-17

### Added

- Storm mode: river currents, gusts, floods and the storm sky.

## [1.2.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.1.0...v1.2.0) - 2026-10-17

### Added

- Rush Hour mode: events and waves of traffic, fog and the rain slide.

## [1.1.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/compare/v1.0.0...v1.1.0) - 2026-10-17

### Added

- Last Train mode: trains on the line, the hop combo and a day and night
  cycle.

### Removed

- The skeleton's unused title tune from the sound test.

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/comprofix/nightcrossing/releases/tag/v1.0.0) - 2026-10-16

### Added

- Stroll mode: hop the toad across the road and the river to the five goal
  nooks, with lives, a timer, scoring and levels.
- Night lighting, particles and animation, music and sound effects.
- The mode menu, intro, title demo, and glow, CRT and high contrast options.
- A speed ceiling of 225%, reached at level 38.
- A fallback for graphics cards without shader support.
